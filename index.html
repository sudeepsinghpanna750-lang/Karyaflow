FAQ

Is it really free to start?

Yes — every new account gets 5 workflows free, forever. No card required.

Which payments do you support?

Razorpay (UPI, Cards, Netbanking in INR) and Stripe (global cards in USD).

Do you store my password?

Never in plain text. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and sessions use HTTP-only cookies.

Can I use my own AI model?

Yes. Point KaryaFlow at any OpenAI-compatible endpoint via environment config.
